# Attributions

compare-idioms tests idioms that other people found, wrote down and
collected. This file credits each collection and records the terms it is
published under.

How credit is given here:

- Every idiom in the catalog names its source and the source's own
  identifier for it. Every reg-rs test for that idiom repeats the credit
  in its description.
- This file groups idioms by source, using this project's idiom ids only.
  It does not reproduce a source's list: not its order, its numbering,
  its headings, its descriptions or its expressions.
- The catalog is organized by this project's own ids and categories. An
  idiom is included because it is tested across languages here, never
  because a source is being imported.

The license notes below are what was found on 2026-10-06, not legal
advice. "Not found" means no statement was located, not that none exists.
